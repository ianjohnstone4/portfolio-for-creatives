ISSUE 1: ISOLATION & LACK OF COMMUNITY
Remote workers and students often feel disconnected from others. Virtual work
environments can feel especially lonely and silent.
Users get low-pressure, meaningful social interaction while still being productive.

Cospace Solution:
In-Person:

• Browse and join nearby coworking meetups

• Structured sessions with light, low-pressure social
breaks.


Virtual:

• Join virtual coworking rooms with and shared focus
cycles

• Light voice-optional or camera-optional presence

<PARSED TEXT FOR PAGE: 18 / 20>

ISSUE 2: DIFFICULTY MAINTAINING FOCUS &
ROUTINE

Users struggle to start tasks, stay focused, and maintain a productive rhythm on their own.
Cospace Solution:
In-Person & Virtual Features:

• Guided focus blocks

• Scheduled breaks and lunch periods

• Focus timer with visual progress bar

• Start-of-session goal setting

• End-of-session reflection and
progress summary
CoSpace adds external structure to an otherwise unstructured day, helping users enter and exit focus
states more easily.

<PARSED TEXT FOR PAGE: 19 / 20>

ISSUE 3: PROFESSIONAL/ACADEMIC ENGAGEMENT

Remote work reduces spontaneous interaction with peers, limiting networking, shared
learning, and motivation.
Cospace Solution:
In-Person:

• Industry-based coworking groups

• Easy face-to-face engagement during breaks


Virtual:

• Field-specific virtual coworking rooms

• Light chat for shared progress and questions
Reintroduces peer connection and informal professional learning into
remote work and study environments.
