# SharedStandard motion clips

Prepared for Ian Johnstone's portfolio on 2 November 2026 from his supplied
[IXD 203 animation file](https://www.figma.com/design/YCVrT1nOZuq9tiEGy8fn5d?node-id=0-1).
These are a later individual motion study, separate from the original IXD 105
team project and its usability testing.

- `open-dishes.mp4`: direct recording of the original prototype, frames
  `1:113` → `1:177` → `1:241` → `1:278`. Shows selection, the selected card's
  movement into the detail view, and the criteria reveal.
- `custom-timing.mp4`: direct recording of the original prototype, frames
  `1:355` → `1:404` → `1:455` → `1:506` → `1:562`. Shows custom controls opening
  and the proposed deadline remaining after Apply deadline.
- `send-for-review.mp4`: native Figma Motion export of `1:662`. The original
  timeline is 2 seconds, while its existing keyframes finish at about 3.12
  seconds. A temporary clone used a 4.5-second export duration so the complete
  existing sequence and final status are visible. No artwork, text, keyframes,
  easing, or original timing was changed. The temporary clone was removed and
  the original 12 source frames were preserved.

The first two recordings retain the prototype's actual elapsed transition
timing, with brief holds for reading. Only browser chrome and black margins
were cropped. All clips are silent H.264 MP4, 786 × 1854 pixels. WebP posters
come from each clip's initial frame. No generated research or measured
usability outcome is implied by the accompanying purpose descriptions.

Playback uses the original MP4 files as silent, continuously looping previews.
JavaScript replaces native video controls with one accessible pause/play button
above the group. Clips load and play when at least 15% visible, and pause when
off screen or when the page is hidden. Reduced-motion preferences start the
previews paused; visitors can explicitly choose to play. A blocked autoplay
attempt presents the play button. Native controls remain available without
JavaScript, and a failed clip exposes a direct file link. Original footage and
animation timing are unchanged.
